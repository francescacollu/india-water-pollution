## A problem of scale

Placeholder: how big is the problem? Rivers, sewage, and the gap between what is built and what works.

[[scrolly graphic="sewage-map"]]
[[step view="generation"]]
Indian cities generate about **72,000 million litres of sewage every day**. The darker the state, the more sewage it produces.
[[note: confirm CPCB source year for the state CSV]]
[[step view="capacity"]]
Treatment plants have been built to handle only **44%** of that volume. Some states have almost no capacity at all.
[[step view="treated"]]
And what is actually treated is lower still: roughly **28%** of the sewage generated.
[[step view="efficiency"]]
The plants that exist are not working at full capacity either: on average they treat about **64%** of what they were built for.
[[/scrolly]]
